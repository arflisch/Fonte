## Notes for App Review (English)

Fonte is an offline strength training log with optional nutrition tracking. No account is needed.

- Fonte Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase: Settings (top right of the Workouts tab) › Fonte Pro › Unlock. "Restore my purchases" is on the same page.
- Pro features to look at once unlocked: the Nutrition tab (calorie target, food search, barcode scan, dishes, water, fasting), automatic progression in Programs, Apple Health in Settings, advanced sets in a workout.
- Apple Health (Pro, off by default, Settings › Apple Health): workouts, food energy and macros, and water are written to Health; body weight and active energy are read from it. Health data stays on the device and is never used for advertising.
- Barcode scanning uses the camera only to read EAN/UPC codes. Unknown products are looked up on Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), sending only the barcode or the search words.
- Import (Settings › Import from Strong or Hevy) reads a CSV export from those apps on the device.
- Calorie targets are estimates based on the Mifflin-St Jeor formula; the app says they are not medical advice.
- Encryption: backups use the platform's built-in cryptography only (exempt).
